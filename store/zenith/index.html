What it does

Full admin layout: sidebar with a weather card, top bar with a day switch, 4 KPI cards with sparklines, sun chart, energy flow, weekly chart and a panel-strings table
Hand-drawn sun chart (no chart library): the sun travels its real arc from sunrise to sunset and draws the day's solar output and site usage beneath it
Clouds sit on the sun's path, the sun dims as it passes under them and the output curve dips with it; today shows a Now marker and a forecast line
Energy flow diagram between solar, site, battery and grid with lines that run faster when more power flows; battery, self-powered % and savings are worked out from your data
Weekly made-vs-used bars, a sortable and searchable strings table with performance bars, hover or arrow-key readouts and light and dark themes
Responsive down to 390px: icon rail on tablets, slide-out drawer on phones, zero dependencies

Use it in 2 minutes

  1. Unzip and copy the CSS and JS into your project.
  2. Paste the markup where you want it.
  3. Change the CSS variables to match your brand.
<link rel="stylesheet" href="zenith.css">
<div class="zenith" data-zenith data-zenith-manual>
  <!-- sidebar, KPI cards, sun chart and flow from index.html -->
</div>
<script src="zenith.js"></script>
<script>
  const dash = Zenith.mount(document.querySelector('[data-zenith]'), {
    day: 'today',
    data: mySolarData   // same shape as Zenith.data
  });
  dash.setDay('yday');
</script>

Questions

Can I plug in my own data?

Yes. Pass a data object to Zenith.mount() with your site settings, one entry per day (solar and usage power every 15 minutes, battery level at 6 am, clouds and weather), a week of totals and your panel strings. Zenith.data shows the exact shape, and battery, savings and energy flow are calculated for you.
