What it does

Full admin layout: sidebar with a water-tank card, top bar with a greenhouse switch and Run irrigation button, 4 KPI cards with sparklines, the irrigation map, yield donut with growth stages, 14-day soil moisture chart, irrigation log, beds & sensors table and a water-use chart
Hand-drawn irrigation map (no library): a tank, pump and valve feed a main pipe and 4 drip lines over 32 plants, and a glowing water pulse with a light trail branches into every line in a left-to-right wave
Every plant reacts as the water reaches it: droplets fall from its emitter, a splash ring spreads on the soil and it springs up to its growth stage while its moisture gauge fills (healthy green, dry amber that droops first, dashed-blue waterlogged, hatched offline sensor)
Live run stats (litres delivered, plants watered, tank level, issues), a draining tank, KPIs that roll, an event log that fills as the water finds problems, and charts that re-draw in a wave on every switch
Hover, tap or arrow-key through plants for bed, crop, moisture, growth stage and last watering; click a table row to light up its bed on the map; crosshair tooltips on the moisture and water charts
Light and dark themes, responsive down to 390px (icon rail on tablets, slide-out drawer on phones, the map scrolls inside its card), reduced-motion support, zero dependencies

Use it in 2 minutes

  1. Unzip and copy the CSS and JS into your project.
  2. Paste the markup where you want it.
  3. Change the CSS variables to match your brand.
<link rel="stylesheet" href="sprout.css">
<div class="sprout" data-sprout data-sprout-manual>
  <!-- sidebar, KPI cards, irrigation map and table from index.html -->
</div>
<script src="sprout.js"></script>
<script>
  const farm = Sprout.mount(document.querySelector('[data-sprout]'), {
    data: myGreenhouses   // same shape as Sprout.data
  });
  farm.setZone(1);  // the water runs through House B
</script>

Questions

Can I plug in my own sensor data?

Yes. Pass Sprout.mount() a data array with one entry per greenhouse: name, crop, climate, today's water use and target, tank level, and up to 4 beds ({ id, name, shape, yield, ec, ph, temp }) with 8 plant readings each ({ moisture, growth, litres }, moisture null = sensor offline) plus 14 days of [moisture, temp, litres]. Feed it from your IoT platform, MQTT bridge, a spreadsheet export or any JSON and call setZone() or water() to run the irrigation again.
