What it does

Every page is a bead on one rod: the beads around the current page swell into readable numbers while far pages shrink to dust, so 24 or 50 pages fit on a phone
Changing page launches a glowing bead down the rod with a light trail; the fisheye swell travels with it and every bead it passes hops and lights up
It lands with a clack: the bead squashes, a shockwave ring pops and the neighbouring beads get knocked aside and spring back
Tap any bead or anywhere on the rod, drag along it to scrub with the swell under your finger, or use the arrow posts (they bump at the ends)
Caption with a rolling page number and a '17–20 of 94 entries' range, onChange/onSettle callbacks, go()/next()/prev()/setTotal() API
Real nav and buttons, aria-current, roving tabindex with arrow/Home/End/PageUp keys, live announcements, reduced-motion friendly, zero dependencies

Questions

How many pages can it handle?

Each page is a bead, and it reads best up to about 60 pages: the swell always keeps the current page and its neighbours big enough to read and tap while the rest shrink to dust. Change the page count any time with setTotal(), for example after a search.
