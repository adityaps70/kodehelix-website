FAQ

Questions people ask us.

How long does a website take?

A Launch site is ready in 7–10 days, a Business site in 3–4 weeks and a store or booking site in 4–6 weeks. Web apps and platforms are planned in milestones after a discovery call.

Do I see the design before paying for everything?

Yes. We design your homepage first. You approve the look before we build the rest, so there are no surprises.

Can I edit the website myself later?

Yes. Business and Commerce sites come with an admin panel, and every project includes a short training session. If you'd rather not, our Care plan covers edits for you.

Do you handle domain, hosting and email?

We set them up in your name, so you always own them. Domain and hosting are billed at cost by the provider; we don't mark them up.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every site is built with on-page SEO basics, fast loading and Google Analytics. Business sites also include a Google Business Profile setup, which helps most for local searches.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We've built for a client in Singapore and work with businesses worldwide. International pricing is in US dollars.
