# Link plan

kodehelix.in is one month old with no known links. Google won't rank a new domain for "website design company in lucknow" (competitors have 10+ years and hundreds of reviews) on content alone. Order of work:

1. **Owned and client links (week 1):** client-site footer credits (backlog #6), LinkedIn, Instagram bio, GitHub org, Behance, Dribbble.
2. **Directories (weeks 1–2):** `directories.md`. Clutch and GoodFirms matter most: they're cited by AI assistants for "best agency in <city>" questions.
3. **Lists (weeks 2–4):** sites already ranking for "top website design companies in Lucknow" (e.g. webvoomlucknow.com blog, orangeitsoftwares.com top-10, GoodFirms/Clutch city lists). Most are self-serving agency lists that won't add a competitor; the directory ones (GoodFirms, Clutch, DesignRush) are realistic.
4. **Maritime niche (month 2):** maritime publications and associations that list service providers. Marine Tanks and Sea N Shore are already in this world: an "our technology partner" mention on each is a natural, relevant link.
5. **UI store (ongoing):** CodePen and DEV.to posts are where dev links come from. One good tutorial post a month beats 20 directory listings for /store/.

Never buy links or use link farms: a new domain can't absorb a penalty.
