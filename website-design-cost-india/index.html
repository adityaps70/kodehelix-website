What changes the price

Biggest factor

Custom design vs a template

A bought theme with your logo is cheaper up front, but looks like thousands of other sites and is often slow on phones. Every Kodehelix site is designed for the business.

Scope

Number of pages

Each service, location or product category usually deserves its own page, both for customers and for Google. 1–5 pages is Launch; 8–15 is Business.

Features

Bookings, payments, logins

Anything that stores data or takes money (bookings, Razorpay or Stripe, customer accounts) moves you from a brochure site to a store or web app.

Content

Who writes and shoots

Good photos and clear text matter more than animation. If you have them ready, the build is faster.

Running costs people forget

  • Domain: renewed every year with the registrar. We register it in your name and bill it at cost.
  • Hosting: from free (simple static sites) to a monthly fee for stores and web apps. Billed at cost by the provider.
  • Payment gateway fees: Razorpay, Cashfree and Stripe charge a percentage of each transaction. Check their current pricing pages.
  • Business email: Google Workspace or Zoho Mail charge per user per month.
  • Upkeep: updates, backups and small edits. Our Care plan is ₹5,000 a month; Growth (adds monthly SEO and Google posts) is ₹10,000 a month. Both are optional.
