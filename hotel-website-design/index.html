Why direct bookings matter

Booking sites such as Booking.com, Agoda and MakeMyTrip bring guests, but they charge a commission on every booking. Your own website won't replace them, but every repeat guest, referral or Instagram follower who books through your site instead is a booking you keep in full, and you get the guest's number to invite them back.

The guest usually finds you on Instagram, Google Maps or a booking site, then searches your name. A website that answers their questions and makes booking one tap is what turns that search into a direct booking.

Can guests book and pay online?

Yes. Most of our hotel clients start with a booking form that sends dates to WhatsApp, which needs no extra fees. When you want deposits or full payment online, we add Razorpay, Cashfree or Stripe.

Do you connect to a channel manager or booking engine?

If you already use one, we link or embed it on your site. If you don't, we recommend starting with WhatsApp booking and adding one when your volume needs it.
